Methodology & Limitations

All data from Polar H10 chest strap via Bluetooth, sampled at RR-interval resolution and aggregated into 5-second windows. RMSSD values above 150ms are excluded as likely artifacts. Moving averages use a centered 30-sample window (~2.5 minutes). Correlation coefficients are Pearson product-moment. Recovery time is defined as the first 5-sample window exceeding 90% of the pre-intervention baseline. Movement data covers only 1.6% of the session due to phone-based accelerometer limitations.
