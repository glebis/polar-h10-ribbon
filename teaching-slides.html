Why does this work?

Flow state suppresses the Default Mode Network — the brain network responsible for self-referential thinking, rumination, and worry. When you're fully absorbed in teaching, the DMN quiets. Anxiety requires self-focus; flow eliminates it.

Polyvagal theory (Porges, 1994): Teaching activates the ventral vagal circuit — the social engagement system. Voice modulation, eye contact, reading the room — all vagal. This is the only autonomic circuit compatible with calm, connection, and learning.

Csikszentmihalyi's flow: When challenge matches skill, anxiety (too much challenge) and boredom (too little) both disappear. The HRV data confirms this physiologically — the autonomic nervous system enters its optimal state exactly when the psychological conditions for flow are met.

Peifer (2012): flow correlates with increased vagal tone and decreased cortisol.
