# What My Heart Data Tells Me

## The Setup

Polar H10 chest strap streaming ECG, heart rate, and beat-to-beat intervals over Bluetooth to a custom Python daemon. 45,000+ heartbeats recorded over 11.4 hours — from late evening through sleep to morning exercise.

## The Numbers That Matter

**Resting RMSSD (standing): ~22-25ms**
This is my baseline heart rate variability — how much my heart rhythm fluctuates between beats. For context, healthy adults average 30-45ms seated. Mine is on the low side, consistent with a GAD diagnosis. The vagus nerve — the main parasympathetic brake on the heart — runs quieter in anxious systems.

**DFA α1: 0.82**
This measures the fractal complexity of my heartbeat. A healthy heart doesn't beat regularly or randomly — it has a specific self-similar pattern, like a coastline. 0.82 is in the healthy range (0.75-1.25). Below 0.75 is the strongest known predictor of cardiac mortality from HRV data.

**1 minute of coherence breathing: +34% RMSSD**
Breathing at 5.5 seconds in, 5.5 seconds out made my heart rate swing between 61 and 101 bpm — a 40 bpm oscillation driven entirely by the vagus nerve engaging and releasing with each breath cycle. This is called respiratory sinus arrhythmia, and the size of that swing is a direct measure of vagal tone.

A +34% acute response puts me in the "strong responder" category. Research (Vaschillo et al. 2006) shows people with larger acute responses gain more from long-term training.

## What Cannabis Does (Measured)

Smoked at ~01:19. The data shows:
- Acute: RMSSD crashed within 30 seconds
- 2-3 hours later: a 1-hour block of RMSSD stuck at 7-12ms during sleep (03:35-04:30). This is THC suppressing deep sleep architecture.
- Net effect: overnight HRV average reduced, 20 low-HRV fragmentation blocks during sleep. Sleep quality rated "poor" by the algorithm.

## What Exercise Does (Measured)

3-minute bodyweight session, HR peaked at 164:
- RMSSD crashed to 6ms during exercise (expected)
- Parasympathetic rebound: RMSSD rose to 40ms within 3 minutes — 73% above my pre-exercise baseline
- Coherence breathing immediately after pushed it to 45ms
- The combination of exercise + breathing nearly doubled my resting RMSSD

## What This Means Practically

My vagus nerve works. It responds strongly to breathing training (+34% in 60 seconds). It responds to exercise with a healthy rebound. The fractal structure of my heartbeat is intact (α1 = 0.82).

The low resting baseline isn't damage — it's a nervous system tuned for vigilance (GAD). The research says 10 minutes of daily coherence breathing at my resonance frequency should raise that baseline by 15-30% over 4-6 weeks. Exercise + consistent sleep timing compound the effect.

The single biggest measurable drag on my autonomic recovery: cannabis before sleep. One session produced a 1-hour hole in my overnight HRV that no amount of breathing training can compensate for.
